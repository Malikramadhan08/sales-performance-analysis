---

## 📖 Project Overview

The goal of this project is to transform raw sales transaction data into an interactive dashboard that provides an overview of business performance.

The dashboard analyzes sales trends, profitability, regional performance, category performance, and top-selling products to help identify key business trends and areas that may require further attention.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Clean and prepare raw sales data for analysis.
- Analyze sales and profit performance across different business segments.
- Build a professional dashboard using Microsoft Excel.
- Visualize sales trends and business performance through PivotCharts.
- Generate meaningful business insights and recommendations from the analysis.

## 📈 Dashboard Features

- KPI cards displaying Total Sales, Total Profit, Total Orders, and Profit Margin.
- Monthly Sales Trend showing sales performance from 2023 to 2026.
- Sales by Category to compare performance across product categories.
- Profit by Sub-Category to identify the most and least profitable segments.
- Sales by Region to compare regional performance.
- Top 5 Products by Sales to identify the highest-selling products.
- Clean dashboard layout designed for easy interpretation.

---

## 💡 Key Insights

- **West** was the strongest-performing region, generating the highest sales and profit among the four regions.
- **Technology** was the highest-selling product category.
- **Copiers** generated the highest profit among sub-categories, with approximately **$56.1K** in profit.
- **Tables** recorded a negative profit of approximately **-$17.8K**, making it the weakest-performing sub-category from a profitability perspective.
- **Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier** was the top-selling product, generating approximately **$61.6K** in sales.
- Overall, the business generated approximately **$2.33M in sales** and **$292.3K in profit**, resulting in a **12.22% profit margin**.

---

## 💡 Recommendations

- Investigate the negative profitability of the **Tables** sub-category by reviewing product-level sales, discounts, pricing, and costs.
- Analyze the factors contributing to the **West region's** strong performance and identify opportunities to apply similar strategies to other regions.
- Continue monitoring **Technology** products due to their strong sales contribution while also evaluating their profitability.
- Review high-selling products such as the **Canon imageCLASS 2200 Advanced Copier** to identify opportunities for sustained sales growth.
- Focus on improving low-profit segments to support an increase in the overall profit margin.
